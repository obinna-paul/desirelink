Most social discovery apps ask you to swipe first and figure out compatibility later, usually a few awkward messages in. Udala gives people more useful signals before a conversation begins, including shared interests, communities, location preferences, and availability.

That is the short version of what makes Udala different. Here is the longer one.

## A real-time social marketplace

Udala brings several connected experiences together:

- **A discovery layer** — private Preferences that help make people, posts, and communities more relevant.
- **A presence layer** — Available Tonight, which shows who's actually free and open to something right now, not just who has an account.
- **A creator layer** — Creator Menus, where people who make content or offer services can set up tiers, subscriptions, and private access on their own terms.

You can use all three, or just one. Plenty of members join purely to follow a few creators without ever turning on an availability status.

## Getting started

When you sign up, you can start with the essentials and add Preferences later from Edit Profile. Your choices are private by default, nothing is locked in, and you can change them at any time.

From there, the Home feed adapts to what you've told us: matches who share your interests, and creators whose menus line up with what you're looking for.

Over the next few posts on this blog, we will go deeper into each part of the platform: making Preferences useful, understanding availability, building an audience, offering services, and staying safe.

If you are new here: welcome. Set up the parts of your profile that matter to you, see who is around, and take it from there.
