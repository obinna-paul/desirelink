Most social discovery apps ask you to swipe first and figure out compatibility later, usually a few awkward messages in. Udala brings public posts, live activity, communities, services, and nearby people into one place.

That is the short version of what makes Udala different. Here is the longer one.

## A real-time social marketplace

Udala brings several connected experiences together:

- **A discovery layer** — search, hashtags, location, and real activity that help people and creators find one another.
- **A presence layer** — Available Tonight, which shows who's actually free and open to something right now, not just who has an account.
- **A creator layer** — Creator Menus, where people who make content or offer services can set up tiers, subscriptions, and private access on their own terms.

You can use all three, or just one. Plenty of members join purely to browse a few creators' menus without ever turning on an availability status.

## Getting started

When you sign up, you can start with the essentials and add more profile details whenever you are ready.

From there, the Home feed learns from real actions such as viewing, liking, saving, commenting, sharing, and subscribing. Discover also uses privacy-aware location, availability, verification, and recent activity to make results more useful.

## What's next

Over the next few posts on this blog, we will go deeper into each part of the platform: understanding availability, building an audience, offering services, and staying safe.

If you are new here: welcome. Set up the parts of your profile that matter to you, see who is around, and take it from there.
