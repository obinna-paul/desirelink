Preferences help Udala understand the people, conversations, communities, and experiences you want to find. They improve recommendations without turning your profile setup into a long questionnaire.

## What you can choose

The Preferences flow asks three straightforward questions:

1. **What are you hoping to find?** Choose the kinds of connections or experiences you want now.
2. **What do you usually enjoy?** Select the topics and activities that tend to interest you.
3. **What should we avoid showing you?** Set clear boundaries for recommendations.

You can choose as much or as little as feels useful. A few honest selections are more helpful than choosing everything.

## Private by default

Your selections are private by default. They can improve what Udala recommends without appearing publicly on your profile.

If you want to share selected preferences as public signals, you can turn that on during the final review. You remain in control of what other people can see.

## How preferences affect Udala

Preferences help improve:

- The people and communities suggested in Discover.
- The order of relevant public content in your feed.
- Suggestions that better respect your interests and boundaries.

They work alongside signals such as location preferences, availability, and recent activity. No single preference determines everything you see.

## Change them whenever you need

What you want can change. Open **Edit Profile → Preferences** whenever you want to update your selections or change what is visible on your profile.
